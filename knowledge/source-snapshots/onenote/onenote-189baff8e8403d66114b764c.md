\4) Keep the following matters when working in the pit.

a) Turn off the pit switch when you do not need to operate the car.

b) Set the pit ladder in the case the movable pit ladder is applied.

c) Turn NORMAL/INSPECTION switch of Pit inspection device to INSPECTION side before getting work started.

NOTE：For restoring to NORMAL mode, turn the key switch of the hall reset device to RESET side with closing the hall/pit doors and turning on the safety switches around pit. Therefore, take care when you set INSPECTION mode in the pit while

installing.\n
