| **Why Lever Slack Is Required** **Adjust the wire rope length so that the lever has 2–3 mm of slack.** This slack is necessary to allow the interlock mechanism to return completely to its normal position. If there is no slack, the lever may remain slightly pulled, and the interlock may not fully lower. As a result, the system may incorrectly detect that the landing door is open, even though the door is physically closed. In this condition, the elevator cannot operate because the door safety circuit is not properly established. In some cases, if this open-door detection occurs unexpectedly, it may lead to passenger entrapment. Therefore, always confirm that the lever slack is adjusted to 2–3 mm. |
