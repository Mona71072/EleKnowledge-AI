| **High-Frequency Noise Countermeasures** High-frequency noise may be generated by the high-speed switching operation of the VVVF inverter. This noise can affect other circuits through power lines, communication lines, and grounding paths, causing communication errors or unstable operation. The line filter reduces noise on the power line, and the isolation transformer helps reduce noise influence by electrically separating the primary and secondary sides. During inspection, confirm that the wiring is not damaged, terminals are not loose, grounding is properly completed, and no conductive foreign objects such as metal fragments remain inside the panel. | [OneNote attachment reference omitted; attachment not copied] |
