戸開走行保護装置ブレーキ確認（動的トルクBK1/BK2)

Unintended Car Movement Protection (UCMP) Brake Confirmation (Dynamic Torque BK1/BK2)

(1)作業内容

Work Procedure

①片ブレーキを引き摺りながら低速自動運転を行う。

Perform low-speed automatic operation while dragging one brake.

②左右（BK1、BK2）共に実施し、走行中のトルクが乗場インジケーターに表示される。

Perform this for both left and right (BK1, BK2) brakes, and the running torque will be displayed on the landing indicator.

(2)目的

Purpose

①走行中にブレーキを掛け、カゴが適正に停止できるかを確認する。

Confirm whether the car can stop properly by applying the brake during travel.

②トルク値に異常がある場合（トルクが弱い、不足している）、急停止を掛けてもカゴが止まるまでに時間がかかる（戸開走行感知時は挟まれの危険性が高くなる）、カゴが突き上げ/突き下げしてしまう等の重大な不具合の原因となる。

If the torque value is abnormal (torque is weak or insufficient), it can lead to serious malfunctions such as the car taking a long time to stop even with an emergency stop (increased risk of entrapment during unintended car movement detection), or the car overtraveling/undertraveling.

(3)インジケーターに表示される値について

Regarding values displayed on the indicator

①ブレーキトルクの出荷時基準値を100％として、本調整で何％のブレーキトルクがあるかを表示している。出荷時基準値の80％以下の場合はNG。

The brake torque's factory standard value is set to 100%, and this adjustment displays what percentage of brake torque is present. If it is 80% or less of the factory standard, it is NG.

②よって、数値が大きいほど良い。

Therefore, a larger value is better.

③基本的には100より大きい値が表示される。

Generally, a value greater than 100 is displayed.

**\[4\] Removing Rust from Brake Drum Surface**

**左側写真のラベル (Photo Labels)**

- **数字が読める状態:** Numbers are legible in the reflection.
- **物体が映る状態:** Objects are reflected.
- **物体が映らない状態:** No reflection of objects.
- **容易に錆が除去できる状態:** Rust can be easily removed.
- **容易に錆が除去できない状態:** Rust cannot be easily removed.

**フローチャートの内容 (Flowchart Translation)**

1. **START: Rust present on the brake drum surface.**  
(ブレーキドラム面に錆あり)
1. **Decision 1: When a steel ruler is placed against the surface, are the reflected numbers on the ruler legible?**  
(鋼尺を当てた際、映る数字が読めるか？)
- *Note: Rust on the edges of the drum surface is acceptable.*
- **YES** → Go to **Step 5 (Verify Torque)**
- **NO** → Go to **Decision 2**
1. **Decision 2: Are objects (e.g., motor cables, hands) reflected on the drum surface?**  
(物体が映るか？)
- **YES** → Go to **Step 5 (Verify Torque)**
- **NO** → Go to **Decision 3**
1. **Decision 3: Can rust be easily removed by wiping with a tightly wrung damp cloth?**  
(水拭きし、容易に錆が除去できるか？)
- *Requirement: The surface must be smooth without catching or resistance (no unevenness).*
- **YES** → Go to **Step 6 (Run-in \[1\])**
- **NO** → Go to **Action:** [4]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) **Remove rust using a nylon abrasive pad or similar.**
1. **Step 5: Verify Brake Torque (Static/Dynamic).**  
(ブレーキトルクを確認する)
- **Within standards (基準内)** → **No maintenance required (手入れ不要)**
- **Outside standards (基準外)** → Go to **Step 6 (Run-in \[1\])**
1. **Step 6:** [4]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) **Remove rust through brake run-in operation.**  
([4]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) ブレーキならし運転で錆除去)
1. **Action after Nylon Pad:** [4]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) **Remove rust and perform "bedding-in" through brake run-in operation.**  
([4]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) ならし運転で錆除去、摺り合わせ)
1. **Final Check: Are the reflected numbers on the steel ruler now legible?**  
(鋼尺の数字が読めるか？)
- **NO** → Return to **Decision 2**
- **YES** → Go to **Step \[3\] (Final Torque Verification)**
1. **Step \[3\]: Verify Brake Torque.**  
(Measure static holding force and dynamic torque to ensure they meet standards.)
1. **Final Results:**
- **Both static and dynamic torque meet standards:**  
→ **Brake torque verified: No issues.** (問題無し)
- **Static/Dynamic torque standards are NOT met:**  
→ **Issue a Trouble Report and contact Inazawa Works (Inaden).** (不発を発行し、稲電に連絡)
