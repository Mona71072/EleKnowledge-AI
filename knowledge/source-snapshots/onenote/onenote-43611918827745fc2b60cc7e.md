Keep the following matters when working on the car top.

a) Turn off the stop switch when you do not need to operate the car.

b) Set the buffer cap to the counter side buffer.

NOTE：For adjusting the car position to ride on the car top, you can use ETP and Pit inspection device. Use the safety fence and don’t set NORMAL mode before the following work c) to avert the troubles around the door.

c) Turn NORMAL/INSPECTION switch of Car top inspection device to INSPECTION side before getting work started.\n
