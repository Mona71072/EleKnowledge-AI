| **Protection Function of the DC Power Supply Uni** |
| A DC power supply unit converts AC power into DC power and is used in various parts of the elevator system. The DC power supply unit has **overcurrent and overvoltage protection functions**. If excessive current flows or an abnormal output voltage is detected, the power supply may stop its output to protect the circuit. **Example** In some elevators, the DC power supply unit is installed inside the power supply box. It converts **200 V AC from H10/H20** into **12 V DC**, and supplies power to the **N2 connector (621H, 601H)** of the landing station. If the protection function operates, power is not supplied to the landing station board, such as **LHH-121XX**. As a result, the landing buttons and hall lanterns connected to the board may not operate. **Important Point** During troubleshooting, if there is no output from the DC power supply unit, the protection function may be active. In this case, first remove the possible cause of overcurrent or abnormal voltage. Then turn off the input power, wait a few minutes, and turn the input power on again. The output voltage may recover automatically. The recovery time may vary depending on conditions such as the input voltage at the time of protection operation. **Remarks** DC power supply units made by manufacturers such as **COSEL** and **TDK-Lambda** are often used. Both manufacturers’ power supply units generally include overcurrent and overvoltage protection functions, although the detailed operation may differ depending on the model. |\n
