**【Reference information】**

| **Why Drip Loop Wiring Is Required** |
| --- |
| Drip loop wiring is used to prevent water from running along the cable and entering the switch box or electrical device. If the cable goes directly into the box without a downward loop, water on the cable may travel along the cable surface and reach the cable entry point. This may not cause an immediate failure, but over time it can become a risk factor for corrosion, poor contact, insulation deterioration, or malfunction inside the switch box. Therefore, as part of quality control, the cable should be routed downward before entering the box so that water drips off before reaching the electrical device. |\n
