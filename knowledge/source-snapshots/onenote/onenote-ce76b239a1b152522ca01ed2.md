| **Check Cable Movement Near Moving Parts** |
| --- |
| In this case, the car door safety shoe cable was damaged and disconnected because the cable routing and fixing position were not appropriate. The cable did not have enough slack near the moving section, so it was repeatedly pulled during door opening and closing. Also, the clip was fixed on the thinner inner cable section instead of the outer black sheath. As a result, stress was concentrated near the fixing point, and the cable eventually disconnected. When checking cables near moving parts, do not only check whether the cable is fixed. Also carefully check whether the cable can move smoothly during door operation without being pulled, bent sharply, pinched, or rubbed. To prevent disconnection, confirm that the cable has enough slack and that it is fixed on the outer sheath without excessive tightening. ⇒[【最終版】中央大学小石川キャンパス不具合報告書提出文書 A版 1.pdf]([URL omitted pending approved OneNote link resolution]) |\n
