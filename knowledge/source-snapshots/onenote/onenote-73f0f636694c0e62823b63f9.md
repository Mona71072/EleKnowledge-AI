**【Reference information】**

| **CAN Communication and Terminating Resistors** CAN communication is used to exchange signals between the control panel and each circuit board. In a CAN wiring line, terminating resistors must be installed at both ends of the communication line. If there is no terminating resistor, the communication signal is reflected at the end of the wiring. This reflection disturbs the signal waveform and may cause communication errors or malfunction. The terminating resistor absorbs the reflected signal and helps stabilize CAN communication. In simple terms, a terminating resistor is a component that helps the CAN signal end properly and keeps the communication stable. | [OneNote attachment reference omitted; attachment not copied] |
| --- | --- |
| **Signal Reflection in CAN Communication** In CAN communication, if the end of the wiring is not properly terminated, the signal may be reflected at the end of the line. This is called **signal reflection**. When reflection occurs, the original ON/OFF square-wave signal becomes distorted and may vibrate. This vibration is called **ringing**. If ringing becomes large, the receiving side may not be able to correctly judge the signal, which can cause communication errors or malfunction. A terminating resistor is installed at both ends of the CAN line to reduce signal reflection and stabilize communication. | [OneNote attachment reference omitted; attachment not copied] |\n
