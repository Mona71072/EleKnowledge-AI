## OneNote content
エレベータ新設工事は、大きく「据付」・「調整」・「検査」の３つの工程に分けられる。

New elevator installation projects are broadly divided into three stages: "Installation," "Adjustment," and "Inspection."

工場（稲沢製作所）で製作・出荷された各機器は現場で前述のとおり3つの工程により、各基準を満足したエレベータが完成し客先に引き渡される。

Each piece of equipment manufactured and shipped from the factory (Inazawa Works) undergoes the three aforementioned stages on-site. An elevator that meets all standards is completed and handed over to the customer.

本研修で解説する「調整」は利用者に直接触れる要素が多く、顧客満足に繋がる非常に重要な工程である。

The "Adjustment" stage, which will be explained in this training, involves many elements directly affecting users and is a crucial process for customer satisfaction.
