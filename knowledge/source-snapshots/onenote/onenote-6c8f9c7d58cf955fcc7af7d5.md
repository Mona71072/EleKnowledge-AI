| Confirm that the motor sheave is properly aligned and that no V-belt wear dust is present around or below the sheave. If the motor sheave is misaligned, the V-belt may run at an angle and rub unevenly against the sheave groove. This can cause abnormal belt wear and generate rubber wear dust. Therefore, V-belt wear dust should be checked as an indication of sheave misalignment or improper belt running condition. |\n
