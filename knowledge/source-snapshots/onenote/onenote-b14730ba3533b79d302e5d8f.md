| **Wire Rope Clip Management for Governor Ropes** |
| --- |
| Wire rope clips for governor ropes must be installed and tightened correctly. If installed properly, wire rope clips can secure approximately **80–85% of the rope breaking strength**. Therefore, in Japan, the following points are controlled as part of quality management. Tighten the wire clips with a torque wrench to the specified torque. After rope tension is applied, retighten the clips to the specified torque. During later maintenance, perform follow-up retightening. Apply slip-detection marking near the wire clips and confirm that the rope has not moved. Incorrect installation or insufficient tightening may cause rope slipping, loss of adjust ment, or safety gear malfunction. |\n
