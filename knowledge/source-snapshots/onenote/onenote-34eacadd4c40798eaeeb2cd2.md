## OneNote content
Set the operation mode select switches as follows

NORMAL/INSPECTION switch \[Car top\] : INSPECTION

NORMAL/INSPECTION switch \[Pit\]  : NORMAL

NORMAL/ED switch  \[Hall\]  : ED

NORMAL/MANUAL switch \[Hall (option)\] : MANUAL

※ In case of MELCO std., there is not NORMAL/INSPECTION switch.

Preparation for sequence test.

1） Set the NORMAL/INSPECTION switch on the car top inspection device to INSPECTION side.

2） Turn on the switches POWER in the car station and set the stop switch to ON (NOT stop).

3） Remove the phase -U, V, W of motor wires and tape them.

4） Remove brake wire conector "DM" in control panel.\n
