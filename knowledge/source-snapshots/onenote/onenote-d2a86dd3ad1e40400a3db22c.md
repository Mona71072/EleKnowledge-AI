Precautions of brake release operation.

\1) For some reason, you need to move the car by brake release operation,

do the following procedure.

a) Turn off the Main swtich (power off).

b) Confirm if the car and landing doors are closed.

c) Switch the ETP work light on.

d) Turn NORMAL/ED switch on ETP to ED side.

e) Turn NORMAL/RESCUE switch on ETP to RESCUE side.

*When NORMAL/RESCUE switch is turned to RESCUE side, the

#DYN contactor for dynamic brake turns off. And, when BKD button

is pressed, the brakes are driven by the QB1 card.

f) Perform SELF-TEST (Press simultaneously the buttons (RUN and UP)

and watch the LEDs 5, 29 and 41DG).

※Turn on the Main switch (power on) again when the battery is not

still connected, such as during installation.

・If the LEDs DO NOT turn ON, there may be some problem in this

sensing system. Therefore, stop rescuing in this way and call for

backup personnel.

・If the all three LEDs 5, 29 and 41DG turn ON release the buttons (RUN and UP) and go to the next step.

g) Push the BKD button to release the brakes with checking the car speed/direction shown by LED on ETP.

CAUTION: Inform passengers before operating the car.

・During the evacuation procedure pay attention to LEDs 5(UP), 29(SPEED), 41DG(DOWN):

\- LED 5 indicates car up-ward movement

\- LED 29 indicates car speed (higher blinking frequency indicates higher speed)

\- LED 41DG indicates car down-ward movement

If car speed is greater than 0.63 m/s (37.8m/min), the LED 29 (SPEED) will be ON (not

blinking), then immediately release the BKD button.

・If LED 5(UP) or 41DG(DOWN) DO NOT turn ON, stop the rescue operation and call for backup personnel.

・If LED 5(UP) or 41DG(DOWN) turn ON watch the LEDs (UP, SPEED, DOWN), move the car

into the door zone of the nearest floor until the DZ (door zone) LED lights up.

h) If the car speed is too slow (nearly BL), attach the weights in the pit to the compensating chain in order to  make the car unbalance.

i) Succeeded to move the car into the door zone?

\<YES> -> NORMAL/RESCUE: NORMAL. Go to next step (evacuation of passengers).

\<NO> -> NORMAL/RESCUE: NORMAL. Stop the rescue operation and call for backup personnel.
